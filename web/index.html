Browsers need a click before they'll play sound.

Play: click the keys, use a MIDI keyboard (Chrome/Edge), or your computer keyboard: the Z–M row plays the bass section and Q–I (with the number row for sharps) plays melody. Sleng Teng: ROCK, START/STOP, then hold D in the bass section (X on your keyboard).

Browser build of the Teng-40 plugin, modelled on the Casio MT-40. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Casio.
